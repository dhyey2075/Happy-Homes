HAPPYHOME FINANCIAL SERVICES — STARTER WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: responsive homepage and enquiry form
- happyhome-logo.png: supplied original company logo used in the header and footer
- styles.css: layout, colours, typography and mobile styling
- script.js: mobile menu, service selection, and WhatsApp enquiry preparation
- privacy.html: draft privacy page that must be reviewed before launch

IMPORTANT:
1. The supplied logo image is included and used in the header/footer. Review its size on desktop and mobile before launch.
2. Review every service description and confirm the company is authorised to offer/arrange each service as described.
3. The form opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; it does not store or submit data to a website server.
4. Review and complete privacy and legal disclosures before going live.
5. Test the WhatsApp number and business contact details.
6. No domain or hosting has been connected by this package.

Quick preview:
Open index.html in a modern browser.

Deployment:
Upload these files to a static hosting provider such as Netlify. After testing the temporary URL, connect happyhomesfinancialservices.in in the hosting provider's domain settings and follow the provider's DNS instructions. Do not change existing DNS records until you know what services currently use the domain.
